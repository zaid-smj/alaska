How to choose a car battery

1. Case size

The new battery must fit the tray and hold-down clamp. Match the size code on your old battery: NS40 for most small cars, N70 for diesel pickups and SUVs.

2. Terminal side

L and R versions are mirror images: the positive (+) terminal sits on opposite sides. Match the letter on your old battery (or bring it to the dealer). With the wrong side, the cables won't reach.

3. Capacity (Ah)

Bigger engines, diesels and cars with heavy AC use need more amp-hours. For extra capacity, choose a higher plate option in the same case size, so it still fits your tray. Going smaller shortens battery life.
