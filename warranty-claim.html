Before You Claim

  • Check the battery is still within its warranty period.
  • Have your warranty card ready. It must be completed, signed and stamped by the dealer at purchase, or the claim will not be accepted.
  • Keep the battery in its original condition. Do not open the battery or remove its electrolyte.
  • Make sure the terminals are clean and tight. Loose clamps often look like a dead battery.
  • A quick test at any Alaska partner can save you a trip.

What Happens Next

  1. Submit your claim

    Send the battery details and the problem using this form.

  2. We contact you

    Our service team replies within two working days with where to take the battery for testing.

  3. Inspection and replacement

    The battery is checked against the warranty terms. Eligible batteries are replaced or repaired.
