Warranty at a Glance

Alaska batteries carry a free-replacement warranty against manufacturing defects. The period depends on the product and is printed on the warranty card supplied with your battery.

  • Dry-charge lead-acid: 6 or 9 months, depending on the card
  • Maintenance-free (MF): 6 or 15 months, depending on the card
  • Lithium (Life Series): 5 years
  • Inverters: 2 years
